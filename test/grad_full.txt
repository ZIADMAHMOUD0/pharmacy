At  the  same  time,  pharmacies  face  challenges  including:   1)  Inefficient  management  of  medicine  inventory  and  orders.  2)  Limited  digital  presence  and  customer  reach.  3)  Difficulty  in  maintaining  organized  records  of  customers  and
